A LITTLE ABOUT US

For the joy, the chaos, and everything in between.

Some days deserve to be remembered exactly as they felt. We make space for the real moments—the quiet glances, the big celebrations, and all the lovely details around them.

Led by Govind Singh Chauhan, our Rohru-based studio has spent more than 30 years documenting life in the hills. From intimate portraits to multi-day weddings, we bring experience, care, and an eye for the moments that matter.
